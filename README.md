# Flavours of Cacao 

## Project Overview

This comprehensive analysis of chocolate and cacao flavors from around the world. The dashboard explores the rich diversity of cacao beans, chocolate production regions, flavor profiles, and quality ratings to provide insights into the global chocolate industry.

## Dashboard Features

### 📊 **Interactive Story Format**
- **Multi-page Navigation**: Story-driven dashboard with sequential analysis
- **Introduction Page**: Overview of cacao industry and analysis objectives
- **Interactive Filters**: Dynamic filtering by region, rating, cocoa percentage
- **Drill-down Capabilities**: Detailed exploration of specific data points

### 🌍 **Geographic Analysis**
- **Global Cacao Origins**: World map visualization of cacao bean sources
- **Regional Comparisons**: Production volume and quality by country
- **Supply Chain Mapping**: From bean origin to chocolate production
- **Climate Impact Analysis**: Geographic factors affecting flavor profiles

### 🍫 **Flavor Profile Analysis**
- **Taste Categories**: Sweet, bitter, fruity, nutty, spicy flavor classifications
- **Flavor Intensity Mapping**: Visual representation of flavor strength
- **Flavor Combinations**: Most common and unique flavor pairings
- **Sensory Analysis**: Professional tasting notes and descriptions

### ⭐ **Quality Assessment**
- **Rating Distributions**: Chocolate quality scores and rankings
- **Expert Reviews**: Professional chocolate taster evaluations
- **Quality vs. Origin**: Correlation between geographic origin and quality
- **Premium Chocolate Identification**: Highest-rated chocolate products

### 📈 **Market Analysis**
- **Price Analysis**: Cost variations by quality and origin
- **Market Trends**: Industry growth patterns and consumer preferences
- **Producer Analysis**: Top chocolate makers and their specialties
- **Cocoa Percentage Impact**: How cocoa content affects ratings and price

## Key Visualizations

### Interactive Maps
- **World Cacao Origins**: Geographic distribution of cacao bean sources
- **Production Heatmaps**: Intensity maps showing major production regions
- **Quality by Region**: Color-coded maps displaying average ratings by country

### Statistical Charts
- **Rating Distributions**: Histograms and box plots of chocolate quality scores
- **Flavor Profiles**: Radar charts showing flavor characteristic intensities
- **Correlation Analysis**: Scatter plots examining relationships between variables
- **Trend Analysis**: Time series charts showing industry evolution

### Comparative Analysis
- **Regional Comparisons**: Side-by-side analysis of different cacao regions

- **Flavor Category Breakdown**: Pie charts and treemaps of flavor distributions
- **Quality vs. Price Analysis**: Value assessment visualizations

## Usage Instructions

### Accessing the Dashboard
1. **Navigate to Tableau Public**: Visit the provided dashboard URL
2. **Story Navigation**: Use story points to progress through analysis
3. **Interactive Exploration**: Click on visualizations to filter and explore
4. **Filter Application**: Use dashboard filters to focus on specific data

### Analysis Workflow
1. **Introduction Review**: Start with Story 1 - Intro for context
2. **Geographic Exploration**: Examine global cacao distribution patterns
3. **Flavor Analysis**: Investigate taste profiles and characteristics
4. **Quality Assessment**: Analyze rating patterns and top performers
5. **Market Insights**: Explore pricing and market trend information

### Best Practices
- **Sequential Navigation**: Follow story sequence for logical analysis flow

- **Tooltip Utilization**: Hover over data points for detailed information
- **Cross-reference**: Compare findings across different visualizations
